# AINGEL Halo — future concept, not implemented hardware

Explore a pendant or clothing clip with wide-angle camera, microphones, speaker, haptics, IMU, large assistance button, privacy indicator and physical camera shutter. Android pairing should reuse backend contracts; investigate Bluetooth/Wi-Fi and optional cellular connectivity after requirements and power measurements.

Concept: two removable battery cartridges and dual charger, plus internal reserve to support battery swaps. Waterproof sealing, thermal load, weight, acoustic feedback and runtime require prototypes. IP67/IP68-class resistance is an engineering target only; no certification exists.

Availability must not imply continuous cloud recording. Investigate local event triggers, a bounded ephemeral buffer, minimal structured memories with consent/retention and discarded irrelevant media. Muse Glimmer is an edge research candidate; benchmark actual hardware/memory/power before any wearable capability claim. Haptic cues and smartwatch/deafblind workflows require participatory testing and do not replace Braille or certified mobility aids.
