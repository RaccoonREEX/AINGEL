## VERIFIED OFFICIAL FACT

The guide requires a public repository, a three-minute demo video and identification of work created during the event. It permits solo entrants and teams up to four. New work must be distinguishable from pre-existing products. Unlicensed assets, policy violations and misrepresented contributions can disqualify a project. Simple image analyzers and basic RAG are listed as anti-projects. AINGEL must demonstrate an integrated assistance workflow.

Judging: impact 30%, Meta tooling 25%, engineering 20%, demo 25%. Meta models must meaningfully participate. The submission form asks for team/members, track, theme, description, public GitHub, video, Meta use/importance and product feedback. It includes a promotional permission checkbox. No submission was made.

The public event lists November 4, 2026, 21:00 ET as deadline. The guide also labels it EST. This maps to 21:00 America/Guayaquil on that date; recheck before submission. Internal delivery target: November 3.

The waiver requires age 18 and local majority, excludes specified jurisdictions/affiliations and requires truthful participation. Entrants retain ownership but grant broad perpetual licenses over submissions/materials. Open-source components are permitted with their licenses respected. A public repository requirement does not itself specify a software license.
